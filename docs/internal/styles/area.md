# Area references

## Observed

[Area Technology](https://www.area.tech/) describes itself as a design studio for
advanced visual technology. Its [OpenAI case study](https://www.area.tech/openai)
organizes the work into named sections and describes the circle as a recurring
symbol across identity and interface explorations. Its [sidebar image](https://pub-c9496d20dacb40f4874f191a2686b638.r2.dev/openai/Sidebar.png)
uses generous spacing, a large serif wordmark, a small uppercase section label,
an outlined action, colored circular markers and a rounded selected row. This
particular image is not evidence for square controls or a grotesk wordmark.

The [Shields case study](https://www.area.tech/shields) presents a design system
through a large series of named badges and frames. The page's image captions
describe a wide range of saturated colors and geometric heraldic motifs; they
do not establish a single restrained palette for all of Area's work.

## Interpretation for Mast

An editorial hierarchy, numbered labels, square controls, hairline rules and
an inverse navigation panel are a proposed adaptation for a dense desktop
application, not claims about every Area project. Theme roles must supply all
colors; the optional Area palettes are independent of the style. A 120 ms
transition and reduced-motion behavior are implementation choices, not motion
observed in the case studies. More visual research is needed before treating
the proposed typography, grids or motion as a direct description of the site.
