- Autonomous workers (child chats whose task spec sets `requiredReport`) get stricter rails. Approvals wake the Manager at once and are declined after `approvalTimeoutSecs` (default 10 min), except host-admin prompts such as `sudo`. Silent or looping workers raise notify-only `workerStalled` wakes. Auto-continue is on by default, and edits outside `ownedPaths` are flagged once in the worker's next report. Interactive and parentless chats are unchanged.
- `report_to_parent` with `completion` stamps the parent's copy with the worker's token totals (`inputTokens`, `cachedInputTokens`, `outputTokens`, `turns`, `toolCalls`).
- ACP agents (Devin, Hermes, Pi, Antigravity) now error out after 45 s with no first response to a prompt, in every chat. Grok keeps 30 s, and `MAST_ACP_PROMPT_STALL_MS=0` turns the bound off.
