- Add the Lead tier to orchestration (Manager → Lead → Worker, local). A Lead is a read-only chat that owns one slice of a project, with at most 4 Leads per project and 6 Workers per Lead, a budget carved from the project's and a path lease over its slice; a Worker of a Worker of a Lead is refused. The Manager hears from a Lead only as a digest of about 350 tokens and never receives its Workers' events. See Manager → Lead → Worker in the orchestrating guide.
