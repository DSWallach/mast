- Settings → Accounts has a **Quota caps** section: pick Balanced failover or Pinned freeze and set the soft, hard (50–95%) and freeze caps per device. They are saved now (`GetQuotaSettings` / `SetQuotaSettings`) and take effect once quota-aware dispatch is on.
- Follow-ups: per-provider caps (the broker only has global ones); whoever wires the broker into dispatch (W4-T2b) loads them with `mast_engine::quota_settings::load`, calls `apply_to` before `QuotaBroker::new` and passes `policy` to `dispatch`, and must decide how a running broker picks up a later save.
- Follow-up: like `SetTitleSettings`, `SetQuotaSettings` is open to local processes on the IPC port; decide on human-only protection before the caps take effect.
