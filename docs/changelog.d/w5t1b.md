- A Lead can run on a paired device. Start it with `create_chat` on your own server (`standingRole: lead`, `workItemId`, `peer`, `peerProject`): your engine admits it like a local Lead (Manager only, four Leads across your device and the paired ones, leases from your outline) and the paired device creates it only on that request. It is read-only, bounded by the same Lead and Worker limits, and reports to your Manager as a 350-token digest that your device re-bounds and checks against your other Leads. A scheduled run inside a Lead's slice now reports to that Lead. A Worker of a Lead no longer reaches the Manager when its Lead is archived, two Leads started together can no longer pass a Lead's six Workers, a Lead scoped to its own subtree can no longer read the whole project's chats, and a Completed Lead frees its slot and lease. Older paired devices are refused (`lead-tier-v1`).
