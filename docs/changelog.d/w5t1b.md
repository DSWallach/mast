- A Lead can run on a paired device. Start it with `create_chat` on the paired device's MCP server (`standingRole: lead`, `parent`, `leadItemId`); it is read-only, bounded by the same Lead and Worker limits, and reports to your Manager as a 350-token digest that your device re-bounds and checks against your other Leads. A Worker of a Lead no longer reaches the Manager when its Lead is archived, two Leads started together can no longer pass a Lead's six Workers, a Lead scoped to its own subtree can no longer read the whole project's chats, and a Completed Lead frees its slot and lease. Older paired devices are refused (`lead-tier-v1`).
