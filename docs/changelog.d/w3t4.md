# W3-T4: worker context packets and `context_read_slice`

- A Worker's `artifact_get_plan` returns its own context packet instead of the whole plan: the Worker role prompt and tool list, the item's instructions, the plan's contract sections, the project-memory rules (from `project-memory.md` in the project's artifact folder) whose scope intersects the task's owned paths, and the plan section that names the task. Structured (JSON) plans are cut by ticket and contract; a plan without sections is returned whole with a note. Other roles still get the full plan.
- Packets are ordered from most static to most dynamic (role prompt and tool list, instructions, frozen contracts, memory rules, task slice; the tool schemas themselves stay in the session's own tool list rather than being repeated), so consecutive tasks of a wave share the longest prompt-cache prefix.
- A packet over its tier cap (Tier 1: 3,000 tokens, Tier 2 and Tier 3: 6,000) is refused with `PacketOverBudget`; the Lead or Manager splits the ticket first.
- Tier-1 packets carry a four-line scaffold (read first, three or more parallel tool calls, test each hypothesis, checkpoint only non-derivable facts). Tier-3 packets get no scaffold and a memory section capped at 200 lines.
- The packet's instructions are the leaf worker's view of its Work Outline instructions: they drop the built-in Manager role entry and entries marked `[orchestration]`. The stored revision and the delivered envelope are unchanged.
- New `context_read_slice { target, maxTokens?, reason }` reads `plan` or `plan#<heading-anchor>` cut at heading boundaries, with a stable `replayId` and a `nextAnchor` when more follows. Each call records a `context_slice_pull`.
- A leaf worker chat (parented, required report, Implementer, TestAuthor or DocWriter) cannot `read_chat` a sibling and may read at most 6 messages of its recorded handoff predecessor. Other roles are unchanged.
