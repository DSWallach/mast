# Respond to Needs you

Open **Needs you** to see requests from chats. Cards show the request, its origin and any supporting detail, links or facts. The Item tab also shows requests for that item and its descendants. Read the linked work before deciding.

Requests can be questions (**ask**), reviews, approvals, choices, handoffs, or notices. Priority controls ordering; notices are informational. Depending on the kind, a card offers an inline reply, options and **Other…**, **Approve** / **Decline**, or **Approve** / **Request changes**. **Dismiss** is available on cards. A reply or decision is relayed to the requesting chat and resolves the request. On a focused choice card, keys `1`–`9` select an option.

Handoff cards can link a branch, diff or PR and show test facts and PR status. A Space can automatically resolve a linked handoff when its PR merges; local merges still need manual resolution.
